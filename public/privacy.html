Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Cavity Audio does not require an account, login, payment, or advertising profile. This policy describes the information processed when you use the hosted service and the local desktop application.

Information processed for hosted simulations

When you submit a hosted simulation, the service processes the simulation inputs needed to perform the job, which can include room dimensions/material settings, source and receiver coordinates, speaker selections, project names supplied by you, and generated simulation artifacts.

Hosted jobs use a temporary capability token. The server stores only a SHA-256 hash of that token, while the raw token is returned to the browser for authorized access to that job.

Operational data

The hosted service may process technical request information such as timestamps, request outcomes, and rate-limit/security events. Security-sensitive values are redacted from application logs. The service is not designed to build an advertising profile.

Retention

Hosted simulations and generated artifacts are intended to be temporary. The current service configuration uses a 24-hour retention window with lifecycle cleanup as a secondary safety net. Local desktop project data remains on the device unless you export or otherwise share it.

Third-party infrastructure

The hosted service may use Google Firebase, Google Cloud Run, Google Cloud Storage, and Google Cloud Tasks to process and store simulation jobs and artifacts. Their handling of information is governed by their own terms and privacy documentation.

Your choices

You may use the local desktop application without sending simulation data to the hosted service. For hosted jobs, do not submit information that you do not want processed as part of the simulation.

Contact and changes

This page is the published privacy notice for the current release. Update it before changing retention, storage providers, or the categories of information processed.
